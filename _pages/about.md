<p class="intro-line">Machine Learning Systems Researcher</p>

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the **University of Pittsburgh**, advised by [**Dr. Longfei Shangguan**](https://shanggdlk.github.io/). My research focuses on efficient systems for generative AI: **LLM serving**, **KV-cache management for video generation**, **GPU resource sharing**, and **multi-agent reinforcement learning**. I work on the runtime mechanisms that connect model execution, memory reuse, and scheduling. I hold an M.S. in Computational Science from the University of Rochester and bachelor's degrees in Computer & Information Science and Mathematics from The Ohio State University.
